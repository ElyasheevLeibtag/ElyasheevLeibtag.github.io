Elyasheev Leibtag

I am a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at Ghent University, hosted by Tom De Medts. My position is supported by the FWO and F.R.S.–FNRS under the Excellence of Science (EOS) programme.

My research is in the geometry and dynamics of infinite groups. I study Lie groups and related groups, including Kac–Moody groups, with a particular interest in their actions on buildings. I also work on symbolic computation and its applications to number theory, including irrationality proofs using conservative matrix fields. I am interested in extending these methods to Feynman integrals in quantum field theory.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Technion, where I was part of the Ramanujan Machine team led by Ido Kaminer. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the Weizmann Institute of Science, advised by Uri Bader. Before that, I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
